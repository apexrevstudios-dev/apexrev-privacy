Privacy Policy

Wagons & Wagers (free) and Wagons & Wagers: Wanted (paid)

Effective 18 October 2026

This policy covers our games Wagons & Wagers (free) and Wagons & Wagers: Wanted (paid), single-player games for Android. We, ApexRev Studios, do not run any servers for them, have no player accounts and do not collect your personal information ourselves.

What stays on your device

The games keep these on your phone only. We never receive them:

  • your campaign saves (including the slots and checkpoints), records and achievements;
  • your sound, appearance (theme and contrast) and tutorial settings, and whether you have already been asked to rate the game;
  • your Tin Tokens balance;
  • if you sign in with Google, your Google name, email address and profile picture (see below).

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes them.

Advertising

The free game shows a small banner ad and offers optional rewarded ads (watch an ad to earn Tin Tokens). Wanted offers optional rewarded ads only, with no banners. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and an Ad privacy choices button in the game lets you change your answer at any time. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Tin Token packs are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. We receive only what Google Play reports about the purchase itself (which pack, and an order reference) so the game can add the tokens.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the game receives your Google name, email address and profile picture from Google. They are kept on your device only, to show who is signed in. We do not receive them, store them on a server or share them. Signing in is never required to play, and you can sign out at any time in the game, which removes them from the device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Rating and sharing

The game may ask, once or twice, whether you would like to rate it. If you agree, Google Play shows its own rating screen; we do not see your rating or who gave it. The Share button opens your phone's share sheet, and nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it.

What you choose to send us

  • Feedback. Settings › Send feedback opens your email app with a message ready to send. Nothing is sent from the game itself. If you send it, we receive your message, the email address you sent it from, and the few details the message includes (the game version, your screen size and a one-line summary of your campaign).
  • Save backup. A backup code is made on your device from your campaigns and records. It is yours to keep or share; anyone who has the code can read those campaigns, so share it with care.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use Settings › Send feedback in the game, or the developer contact on the game's Google Play page.
